ABOUT

Electronics, computing and physics, applied to space systems.

I am an Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering graduate. My interest in physics began at school, where I studied it for two years (Class XI and XII), scored above 80% in both, and did laboratory practicals. I chose this degree because it teaches how physical systems are measured, built and controlled.

My undergraduate research combines electronics, computer science and physics on one problem: how noise and decoherence limit communication and sensing, including in space links. I simulate quantum noise, test error-correcting codes, build sensing hardware, and analyse scientific data.

I am applying to ECE PhD programs where I can continue this work. My formal physics coursework is limited, so I am building that foundation through self-study, which I document publicly.
