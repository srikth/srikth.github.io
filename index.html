ABOUT

Physics is the goal. Electronics and computing are how I work on it.

My interest in physics began at school, where I studied it for two years (Class XI and XII), scored above 80% in both, and did laboratory practicals. I chose Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering because it teaches how physical systems are measured, built and controlled.

Through my undergraduate research I use that training for physics questions: simulating quantum noise and decoherence, testing error-correcting codes, and analysing scientific data. I know my formal physics coursework is limited, so I am building the foundation deliberately through self-study, which I document publicly.

I am applying to graduate programs where I can study quantum systems and noise from the physics side, supported by the engineering and computational skills I already have.
