Questions I want to work on

  • How much does error correction improve performance on realistic noisy links, including long-distance and space channels, once the noise is characterized rather than assumed?
  • Can learning-based estimators characterize quantum noise channels accurately enough to guide the choice of code?
  • How do decoherence, radiation and thermal noise set the design limits of sensing and communication hardware in space?
  • Can physics-informed machine learning combine physical models with data-driven methods to predict the behavior of noisy quantum systems?
  • Do gravity and relativistic motion set fundamental limits on quantum communication and sensing, such as satellite-to-ground links?
  • What physical structure in galaxies can machine learning recover from imaging data, and how reliable are those inferences?
